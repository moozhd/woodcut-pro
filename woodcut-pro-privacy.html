This Privacy Policy explains how WoodCut Pro:Cut List Optimizer (“WoodCut Pro,” “the App,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the App. Project data is stored primarily on your device. We do not operate our own servers for the App. Optional multi-device synchronization may use Apple iCloud and CloudKit.

1. Information You Enter

The App processes information you enter or create to organize woodworking projects and optimize cutting lists. This may include project names, material and board dimensions, part quantities, cutting settings, notes, and generated cutting layouts. This information is used to provide the features you request, save your work, and synchronize it when iCloud synchronization is available and enabled.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in project names or notes unless it is necessary for your own use.

2. Local Storage

Project data and App preferences are stored primarily on your device using the storage provided by Apple’s operating system. Core features do not require a separate WoodCut Pro account. We do not upload your project data to a developer-operated server.

Device backups may contain App data depending on your Apple device and backup settings. Files you export or share, if supported, are stored or handled by the destination you choose.

3. iCloud Synchronization

Where available and enabled, the App may use Apple iCloud and CloudKit to store and synchronize project data across your compatible devices. This involves sending the data needed for synchronization to Apple’s infrastructure associated with your Apple Account.

Use of iCloud depends on your Apple Account, device settings, available storage, and Apple’s service availability. You can manage the App’s access to iCloud through your device’s iCloud settings. Turning off synchronization does not necessarily delete data already stored in iCloud. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Personal Information

You do not need to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or a separate account registration to use the App’s core features. Information you voluntarily place in your projects may nevertheless contain personal information.

If you contact developer support, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide. We use them to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please send only information relevant to your inquiry. Your Apple Account and billing details are managed by Apple.

5. Analytics and Tracking

The current version does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not use your project content for targeted advertising.

Apple may process App Store statistics, usage information, and crash or diagnostic information under its own policies and your sharing settings. Apple may make reports available to developers. Where we receive these reports, they are used to understand reliability and improve the App. You can review applicable analytics-sharing choices in your Apple device settings.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

If paid features or subscriptions are offered, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your complete payment card information. The App may use purchase confirmations and entitlement information supplied by Apple to unlock, verify, or restore paid access.

You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to billing, transaction records, and payment processing.

7. Data Security

We rely on the security protections provided by Apple’s operating system and, when used, Apple’s iCloud infrastructure to help protect App data. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Protect your devices with a passcode, keep system software up to date, and safeguard your Apple Account. Take care when exporting project information or sharing it with others.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: project data remains until you delete it or remove the App’s local data. Use the App’s available deletion controls. Deleting the App generally removes its local data, but offloading the App may retain it.
  • In iCloud: synchronized copies may remain after the App is deleted or synchronization is disabled. Use available App deletion controls and Apple’s iCloud storage-management options to manage cloud data. Deletions may propagate to other synchronized devices when synchronization completes.
  • Backups and exports: backup copies, exported files, and copies shared elsewhere may remain separately and must be managed at their respective locations.
  • Support correspondence: information you send to support is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.
  • Apple records: Apple controls retention of its transaction and service records under its own policies. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.

For questions or applicable privacy requests concerning information you have provided to us, contact developer support as described below.

9. Children’s Privacy

WoodCut Pro is a general-purpose woodworking utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support or a separate App account. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact developer support so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps, including deletion where applicable.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on Apple services, including iCloud, CloudKit, and the App Store. Those services are operated by Apple and are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. If you choose to export or share information with another service, that service’s policies apply to its handling of the information.

For more information, see Apple’s Privacy Policy and App Store & Privacy.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, its services, or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer support contact information listed on the App Store product page for WoodCut Pro:Cut List Optimizer. Open the product page and look for the App Support link or developer support details.

To help identify the App, include its name or Bundle ID: com.hongde.woodcutpro.

1. 您输入的信息

本 App 会处理您为管理木工项目和优化切割清单而输入或创建的信息，例如项目名称、材料与板材尺寸、零件数量、切割设置、备注及生成的切割布局。这些信息用于提供您请求的功能、保存项目，以及在 iCloud 同步可用且已启用时同步数据。

除非您的实际使用确有需要，请避免在项目名称或备注中填写敏感个人信息。

2. 本地存储

项目数据和 App 偏好设置主要通过 Apple 操作系统提供的存储机制保存在您的设备上。核心功能无需单独注册 WoodCut Pro 账号。我们不会将您的项目数据上传到开发者自建的服务器。

设备备份可能包含 App 数据，具体取决于您的 Apple 设备和备份设置。如本 App 支持导出或分享，相关文件将由您选择的目标位置或服务保存和处理。

3. iCloud 同步

在同步功能可用且已启用时，本 App 可能使用 Apple iCloud 和 CloudKit，在您的兼容设备之间存储和同步项目数据。这需要将同步所必需的数据传输至与您的 Apple 账户关联的 Apple 基础设施。

iCloud 的使用取决于您的 Apple 账户、设备设置、可用存储空间及 Apple 服务的可用性。您可以通过设备的 iCloud 设置管理本 App 对 iCloud 的使用。关闭同步不一定会删除已存储在 iCloud 中的数据。Apple 对 iCloud 数据的处理受其自身条款和隐私政策约束。

4. 个人信息

使用本 App 的核心功能无需提供姓名、电子邮箱、电话号码，也无需单独注册账号。但您主动填写的项目内容仍可能包含个人信息。

如果您联系开发者支持，我们会收到您自愿提供的联系方式和相关信息，并使用这些信息回复您的请求、处理问题。请仅提供与咨询有关的信息。您的 Apple 账户和账单资料由 Apple 管理。

5. 分析与跟踪

如本 App 提供付费功能或订阅，相关交易由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会接收或保存您的完整支付卡信息。本 App 可能使用 Apple 提供的购买确认和权益信息，以解锁、验证或恢复付费访问权限。

您可以通过 Apple 账户设置管理订阅。账单、交易记录和支付处理适用 Apple 的相关条款和隐私政策。

7. 数据安全

我们依赖 Apple 操作系统，以及在使用 iCloud 时由 Apple iCloud 基础设施提供的安全保护来协助保护 App 数据。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

8. 数据保留与删除

  • 设备上的数据：项目数据会保留至您将其删除或移除 App 本地数据。请使用 App 中可用的删除功能。删除 App 通常会移除其本地数据，但系统的“卸载 App”（保留文稿与数据）操作可能仍会保留数据。
  • iCloud 中的数据：删除 App 或关闭同步后，云端同步副本可能仍然存在。请通过 App 中可用的删除功能和 Apple 的 iCloud 存储管理选项管理云端数据。同步完成后，删除操作可能同步至其他已启用同步的设备。

  • 支持沟通记录：您发送给开发者支持的信息仅在处理请求和履行适用义务所需的期间内保留。
