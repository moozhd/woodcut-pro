Getting Started

WoodCut Pro helps you organize woodworking projects before making the first cut. A typical workflow is:

  1. Create or open a project.
  2. Add the required parts and enter their dimensions, quantities, materials, and other available details.
  3. Review the cut list for missing or incorrect information.
  4. Add or select sheet materials or stock where supported by your version.
  5. Use available layout or optimization tools to plan your cuts.
  6. Review the result before cutting real material.
Important: Always verify dimensions, blade/kerf allowances, material size, grain direction, and workshop safety requirements before making physical cuts. WoodCut Pro is a planning aid and does not replace safe woodworking practices.

Projects & Cut Lists

Organizing projects

Use projects to keep related parts, materials, and cutting information together. Clear project names make multiple jobs easier to manage.

Creating a cut list

Add each required part and enter the information available in the app, such as dimensions, quantity, and material. Check for duplicate parts, incorrect units, or typing mistakes before relying on a layout.

Editing a project

If your design changes, update the affected parts and review the cutting plan again. A layout based on old dimensions may no longer match the project.

Materials & Cutting Layouts

WoodCut Pro can help you plan how project parts relate to available woodworking material. Depending on the features in your installed version, you may be able to define material sizes and review or optimize cutting layouts.

Make sure the dimensions and quantities in your cut list match the actual material you intend to use.

Real-world results can differ because of saw kerf, trimming, damaged edges, material defects, measurement tolerances, grain requirements, and workshop technique. Confirm the final plan before cutting.

iCloud & Multi-Device Sync

When iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, WoodCut Pro may use Apple's iCloud/CloudKit services to synchronize supported app data between Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

If data is not syncing

  1. Confirm all devices use the intended Apple Account.
  2. Make sure iCloud is enabled and internet access is available.
  3. Check that sufficient iCloud storage is available.
  4. Open WoodCut Pro on each device and allow time for synchronization.
  5. Install current compatible app and operating-system updates when practical.
  6. If synchronization remains delayed, close and reopen the app and check again later.

Synchronization may be affected by iCloud availability, network conditions, device settings, and Apple Account status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a WoodCut Pro account?

The core experience is designed without requiring a separate WoodCut Pro account. Apple services such as iCloud and App Store purchases may require your Apple Account.

Does WoodCut Pro require its own server?

WoodCut Pro is designed around on-device storage and Apple platform services rather than a separate WoodCut Pro project-data server. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, applicable data may be handled through Apple's iCloud/CloudKit infrastructure.

Can I use WoodCut Pro offline?

Features that operate on locally available project data can generally work without a continuous internet connection. iCloud synchronization, App Store transactions, and other Apple online services require network access when communicating with those services.

Why might a real cut differ from the plan?

Check dimensions, quantities, units, material sizes, and supported cutting allowances. Physical cutting also introduces saw kerf, trimming, damaged edges, material defects, and measurement tolerances.

How do I delete project data?

Use the deletion controls provided in WoodCut Pro for the applicable project or item. If data has synchronized through iCloud, deletion may also synchronize to other devices. Confirm that you no longer need the data before deleting it.

I purchased or subscribed, but a paid feature is unavailable.

Confirm that you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If your version provides Restore Purchases, use it. You can also restart the app and check your App Store connection. Billing and transaction processing are handled by Apple.

Purchases & Subscriptions

If your version of WoodCut Pro offers paid features, in-app purchases, or subscriptions, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. WoodCut Pro does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Applicable subscriptions can be reviewed or managed through your Apple Account subscription settings. Refund requests and certain billing matters are handled according to Apple's App Store processes.

Data & Privacy

Your woodworking project information is designed to remain on your devices and, when applicable, in your iCloud environment. WoodCut Pro does not require a separate developer-operated project storage server for its core workflow.

The current product design does not rely on third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. For complete information, please refer to the WoodCut Pro Privacy Policy published with the app's support information.

Troubleshooting

If WoodCut Pro is not behaving as expected:

  1. Confirm important project information is saved where possible.
  2. Close and reopen WoodCut Pro.
  3. Restart your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  4. Check for a newer compatible WoodCut Pro version.
  5. Check for applicable iOS, iPadOS, or macOS updates.
  6. For sync issues, verify your network and iCloud settings.
Before deleting or reinstalling the app: confirm that important data is safely synchronized or otherwise retained. Removing an app can remove locally stored data that has not been synchronized.

开始使用

WoodCut Pro 用于帮助你在实际下料前整理和规划木工项目。典型流程包括：

  1. 创建或打开木工项目。
  2. 添加所需零件，并填写尺寸、数量、材料以及当前版本支持的其他信息。
  3. 检查切割清单，确认没有遗漏或错误。
  4. 根据当前版本提供的功能，添加或选择板材或其他材料。
  5. 使用可用的布局或优化功能规划切割方案。
  6. 实际切割材料之前再次检查结果。
重要提示：实际切割前，请自行确认尺寸、锯缝/刀缝损耗、材料规格、纹理方向以及作业安全要求。WoodCut Pro 是规划辅助工具，不能替代安全的木工作业规范。

项目与切割清单

整理项目

使用“项目”将相关零件、材料和切割信息集中管理。清晰的项目名称有助于同时管理多个木工作业。

创建切割清单

添加每一个所需零件，并填写 App 当前提供的尺寸、数量、材料等信息。使用切割布局之前，请检查重复零件、单位错误或输入错误。

修改项目

如果设计发生变化，请修改对应零件并重新检查切割方案。基于旧尺寸生成的布局可能不再适用。

材料与切割布局

WoodCut Pro 可以帮助你规划项目零件与现有木工材料之间的关系。根据所安装版本提供的功能，你可以设置材料尺寸，并查看或优化相应的切割布局。

请确保切割清单中的尺寸和数量与实际准备使用的材料一致。

实际结果可能受到锯缝、修边、材料破损或缺陷、测量公差、木纹方向以及操作方式等因素影响。切割前请自行核对最终方案。

iCloud 与多设备同步

当当前版本提供并启用了 iCloud 同步时，WoodCut Pro 可能使用 Apple 的 iCloud/CloudKit 服务，在登录同一 Apple 账户的受支持设备之间同步适用的 App 数据。

数据没有正常同步时

  1. 确认所有设备使用的是预期的同一 Apple 账户。
  2. 确认已启用 iCloud，并且网络正常。
  3. 确认 iCloud 有足够可用空间。
  4. 分别打开各设备上的 WoodCut Pro，并留出同步时间。
  5. 在条件允许时安装最新的兼容 App 和系统更新。
  6. 如果仍然延迟，可关闭并重新打开 App，稍后再次检查。

同步可能受到 Apple iCloud 服务状态、网络状况、设备设置以及 Apple 账户状态影响。

常见问题

需要注册 WoodCut Pro 账号吗？

核心使用流程设计为无需另外注册 WoodCut Pro 账号。iCloud、App Store 购买等 Apple 服务可能需要你的 Apple 账户。

WoodCut Pro 是否依赖自己的服务器？

WoodCut Pro 主要围绕设备本地存储和 Apple 平台服务设计，而不是依赖独立的项目数据服务器。启用 iCloud 同步后，适用数据可能通过 Apple 的 iCloud/CloudKit 基础设施处理。

可以离线使用吗？

基于设备本地已有项目数据运行的功能通常不需要持续联网。iCloud 同步、App Store 交易以及其他 Apple 在线服务在通信时需要网络。

为什么实际切割可能和规划结果不同？

请检查尺寸、数量、单位、材料尺寸和当前版本支持的切割损耗设置。实际加工还会受到锯缝、修边、材料缺陷和测量公差等因素影响。

如何删除项目数据？

请使用 WoodCut Pro 中对应项目或数据项提供的删除功能。如果数据已经通过 iCloud 同步，删除也可能同步到其他设备。删除前请确认数据已不再需要。

已经购买或订阅，但付费功能没有生效怎么办？

确认设备登录的是购买时使用的 Apple 账户。如果当前版本提供“恢复购买”，可以尝试恢复购买；也可重新启动 App 并检查 App Store 网络。付款和交易处理由 Apple 负责。

购买与订阅

如果你使用的 WoodCut Pro 版本提供付费功能、App 内购买或订阅，相关交易由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。WoodCut Pro 不会获取或保存你的完整银行卡支付信息。

适用的订阅可以在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中查看或管理。退款申请以及部分账单问题按照 Apple App Store 的流程处理。

数据与隐私

你的木工项目数据设计为保存在设备中，并在适用时存储或同步到你的 iCloud 环境。WoodCut Pro 的核心工作流程不要求使用由开发者单独运营的项目数据服务器。

当前产品设计不依赖第三方广告或跨 App 广告跟踪。完整的数据处理说明请查看与 App 支持信息一同发布的 WoodCut Pro《隐私政策》。

故障排查

如果 WoodCut Pro 没有按照预期运行，可以尝试：

  1. 在条件允许时先确认重要项目数据已经保存。
  2. 关闭并重新打开 WoodCut Pro。
  3. 重新启动 iPhone、iPad 或 Mac。
  4. 检查是否有更新的兼容版 WoodCut Pro。
  5. 检查适用的 iOS、iPadOS 或 macOS 更新。
  6. 如果与同步有关，请检查网络和 iCloud 设置。
删除或重新安装 App 之前：请确认重要数据已经安全同步或通过其他方式妥善保留。删除 App 可能同时删除尚未同步的本地数据。
